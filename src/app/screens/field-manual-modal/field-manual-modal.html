CORE DIRECTIVE
ONE WORD. ONE INFILTRATOR.
All loyal crew members receive the exact same secret word. The Impostor is kept in the dark and only sees the category!
MISSION PROTOCOL
01
INSPECT DOSSIER
Each operative taps their terminal card privately to reveal their role and secret word. Keep your screen protected from view!
02
GIVE BRIEF CLUES
Take turns in real life giving one word or short clue relating to the secret word. The Impostor must bluff and blend in!
03
CROSS-EXAMINE & VOTE
Debate suspicious answers. Vote as a crew on who appears to not know the word.
04
UNMASK THE TRUTH
Tap SHOW IMPOSTOR to reveal the infiltrator and see if the crew prevailed!
AGENT ROLES
LOYAL CREW
KNOWS THE WORD
Give clues that other crew recognize, but subtle enough so the Impostor cannot easily guess the secret word.
THE IMPOSTOR
INFILTRATOR
You do not know the word! Listen closely to earlier clues, bluff convincingly, and divert suspicion!
TACTICAL TIPS
For Crew: Don't make clues too obvious! If the Impostor deduces the word, they can easily echo it.
For Impostor: Stay confident. Blend with generic clues and observe everyone's body language.
